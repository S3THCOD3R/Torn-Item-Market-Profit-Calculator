🛡️ API Terms of Service — How Your Data Is Handled
Category Disclosure
Data Storage Only locally. Listings are rendered in your browser and not stored server-side. The item catalog is cached in your browser's localStorage for 1 hour to reduce API calls.
Data Sharing Nobody. Your data is not shared with any third party.
Purpose of Use Public community tool — item market profit calculation and price screening.
Key Storage & Sharing Stored only in your browser. Transmitted per-request through a stateless proxy; not stored, not logged, not shared.
Key Access Level Public or Minimal. Specifically: market → itemmarket, market → bazaar, and torn → items.

Why a proxy? Browsers block direct cross-origin requests to api.torn.com due to CORS. The proxy is a stateless server-side relay that makes the request on your behalf and returns Torn's JSON response — it does not log, store, or retain your key. Read the entire proxy source: netlify/functions/torn-proxy.js (~110 lines, MIT licensed).

Your key is stored only in your browser and transmitted per-request through a stateless proxy that does not log or retain it. Use a Public or Minimal access key with torn → items permission for name lookup.
